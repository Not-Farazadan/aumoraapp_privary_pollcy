Privacy Policy

Application: Aumora — Romantic Stories & Audiobooks (com.aumora.app)
Effective Date: October 11, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 11, 2026

Welcome to Aumora — Romantic Stories & Audiobooks ("Aumora", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you use the Aumora Android mobile application (package identifier: com.aumora.app) and related backend services (collectively, the "Service").

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with the Google Play Developer Program Policies (including Google Play's User Data Policy, Data Safety disclosures, and Account Deletion requirements), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the app, synchronize your library across devices, process rewards, and deliver your reading and audiobook experience.

Data Category Specific Data Collected Purpose of Collection
Account & Profile Information Email address, display name / username, unique user ID, profile avatar preference, and encrypted authentication credentials (via Email/Password or Google Sign-In). Account creation, authentication, security, and cross-device cloud synchronization.
App Activity & Library Data Bookmarked titles, chapter reading progress, audiobook listening timestamps, unlocked chapters, daily check-in streaks, and virtual wallet balance (Points). Core app functionality, saving reading/listening progress, and restoring unlocked content.
Financial & Purchase History Google Play purchase tokens, order IDs, coin package IDs, and VIP subscription status. We never collect or store credit card numbers or bank account details. Fulfilling in-app coin purchases and VIP subscriptions through Google Play Billing.
Advertising & Device Identifiers Android Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), IP address, basic device model/OS version, and ad reward transaction IDs. Serving optional rewarded video ads via Google AdMob, verifying legitimate ad completion (Server-Side Verification), and preventing reward fraud.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Provide Core Services: To deliver eBooks and audiobooks, remember your last read page or audiobook timestamp, and synchronize your library across devices.
  • Manage Virtual Wallet & Rewards: To maintain your Points balance, unlock chapters, process daily check-in bonuses, and verify rewarded video ad completions.
  • Process In-App Purchases & Subscriptions: To validate coin top-ups and VIP All-Access subscriptions purchased through Google Play Billing.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: To prevent unauthorized access, replay attacks, and fraudulent manipulation of rewarded advertisements or virtual points.
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries, troubleshooting requests, and account deletion requests sent to our support team.

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Aumora integrates trusted third-party service providers to power specific features. We do not sell your personal information to third-party data brokers.

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): Used to display optional rewarded video advertisements when you choose to watch an ad to earn bonus points. AdMob may access your device's Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP address, and coarse interaction signals to serve ads and prevent invalid traffic. When you watch a rewarded ad, your anonymous user ID and a one-time session token are passed to Google AdMob for Server-Side Verification (SSV) so we can securely credit your reward. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In: Used for optional one-tap account authentication and core Android system compatibility.
  • Google Play Billing: All monetary transactions for coin packages and VIP subscriptions are handled directly by Google Play.
  • Cloudflare Workers & D1 Database: Used as our secure cloud infrastructure to store encrypted account profiles, reading progress, and verified reward transaction records.

4. Android Permissions Used

  • android.permission.INTERNET & android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to stream audiobooks, load book chapters, sync your account profile, and load rewarded ads.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Required by Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android 13+ to serve rewarded advertisements and prevent ad fraud. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Data Security & Encryption

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the Aumora app and our backend servers are encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS.
  • Secure Authentication: Passwords are never stored in plain text. Session tokens are protected against unauthorized extraction.
  • Android Backup Protection: Sensitive local authentication data and session storage are excluded from automatic unencrypted Android cloud/device backups.

6. Data Retention & How to Delete Your Account and Data

We retain your personal data only for as long as your Aumora account remains active. In full compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, you can permanently delete your account and all associated personal data at any time using either of the two methods below:

Method 1: Instant In-App Account Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the Aumora app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the More (Profile) tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account & Data.
  4. Tap Delete My Account Permanently to confirm.

Your account, profile, email address, reading/listening history, unlocked chapters, and wallet balance are permanently erased from our database immediately.

Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request (Without Opening the App)

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot access it, send an email from your registered email address to help@aumora.com with the subject line "Delete My Account". We will permanently erase your account and all associated personal data within 7 business days and send you a confirmation email once completed.

Important Note on Subscriptions: Deleting your Aumora account does not automatically cancel an active billing subscription managed by Google Play. To avoid future recurring charges, please cancel any active VIP subscription in the Google Play Store app under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Users)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Access and request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Update or correct your username and profile details directly inside the app.
  • Request permanent erasure of your account and personal data (see Section 6).
  • Opt out of personalized advertising by navigating to your Android device's Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.

8. Children's Privacy

Aumora features romantic fiction and audiobooks intended for teen and adult audiences (16+) and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will delete the account and associated information immediately.
