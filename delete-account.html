Aumora — Delete Account

Application: Aumora — Romantic Stories & Audiobooks (com.aumora.app)

You have full control over your personal data. At any time, you can request the permanent deletion of your Aumora account and all associated personal information, either directly inside the mobile app or through our external support request channel below if you have already uninstalled the app.

How to Request Account Deletion

Option 1 · Instant

Delete Inside the Aumora App

If you still have Aumora installed on your Android device, you can delete your account immediately:

  1. Open the Aumora app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings (More) → Account → Delete Account & Data.
  3. Tap Delete My Account Permanently to confirm.

In-app deletion is processed immediately and permanently erases your account data.

Option 2 · External Request (No App Required)

Request Deletion via Email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, you can submit an external deletion request without opening the app:

  1. Send an email from the address linked to your Aumora account to help@aumora.com.
  2. Use the subject line Delete My Aumora Account and include your registered email address or User ID.
  3. Our team will verify ownership, permanently erase your account within 7 business days, and send you a confirmation email.

Data That Is Permanently Deleted

When your account deletion is completed, the following data is permanently and irreversibly erased from our active databases:

  • Account & Profile Information: Your email address, display name/username, avatar selection, and authentication credentials.
  • Bookshelf & Bookmarks: Your saved books, favorites, and custom library lists.
  • Reading & Listening Progress: All eBook chapter positions, page history, and audiobook playback timestamps.
  • Wallet Balances & Coins: All remaining virtual points, bonus rewards, and daily check-in streaks.
  • Unlocked Chapters: Your chapter unlock entitlements tied to your user profile.

Data Retained for Legal & Anti-Fraud Compliance

In accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable law, a strictly limited set of de-identified or transactional records may be retained solely for legal, tax, financial audit, and security/anti-fraud obligations:

  • Financial & Purchase Logs: Anonymized Google Play Billing order IDs and transaction timestamps required for tax, accounting, and refund verification compliance.
  • Security & Anti-Fraud Records: Cryptographic transaction IDs for rewarded advertisement verification (used strictly to prevent duplicate reward replay fraud), stripped of personal profile information and automatically purged after the statutory retention period (up to 90 days for security logs).

Important Note About Active Subscriptions: Deleting your Aumora account does not automatically cancel an active recurring subscription billed through Google Play. To prevent future billing, please manage or cancel any active subscription in the Google Play Store app under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
