Account Deletion Request

You can request permanent deletion of your Aumora account and associated personal data, even if you have uninstalled the app.

Option 1: Delete Inside the App

  1. Open Aumora and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile or Settings and tap Delete Account.
  3. Review the warning and confirm your request.
  4. Complete any identity verification and follow the on-screen instructions.

Option 2: Request Deletion Here

Click the button below. A popup will ask for your Aumora in-app username and your registered email address. Submit the form to send your deletion request to our support inbox.

Support: bridenook@gmail.com. This is a request, not immediate deletion. We will verify account ownership before processing it.

What Data Is Deleted?

When your deletion request is completed, account-associated personal data will be deleted or de-identified as applicable, including:

  • Account profile and identifying information
  • Bookshelf, bookmarks and reading progress
  • Audiobook listening progress and history
  • Remaining coins and points
  • Account-specific chapter unlocks and entitlements

Data Retention

Limited purchase, security or legal records may be retained where necessary and as explained in Aumora's Privacy Policy. Account deletion may result in loss of access to previously unlocked content.

Important: Never send your password, payment-card details or one-time verification codes. Account deletion is permanent once completed.
